"Selection Runway Shortcuts" in Mathematics

Compare lengthy textbook calculations with Ravi Sir's rapid 5–10 second mental shortcuts:

Exam Problem (Arithmetic / CGL & Airforce Y)

"Find CI on ₹12,000 for 2 years at 12.5% p.a. without using the formula."

Exam Hall Question
Traditional Textbook Method 2.5 to 3 Minutes
1. Write A = P(1 + r/100)^n formula
2. Compute A = 12000 * (1 + 12.5/100)^2
3. Multiply (9/8) * (9/8) = 81/64
4. Long division: 12000 * 81 / 64 = 15,187.50
5. Subtract P: CI = 15,187.50 - 12,000 = ₹3,187.50

⚠️ High risk of calculation errors under exam hall time pressure.

★ Ravi Sir's Selection Shortcut 8 Seconds Mental Calculation
Shortcut Trick: Fraction Ladder (12.5% = 1/8) & Ratio 8:9

Principal = 8^2 = 64 units = ₹12,000. Amount = 9^2 = 81 units. CI = 81 - 64 = 17 units. 17 * (12000/64) = ₹3,187.50 in 2 steps!
